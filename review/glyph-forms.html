Part 2 · the five drawn parts (built)

2 · The five drawn parts built

Noto lacks 龵 龶 㐄 业 𢆉, so they're drawn from KanjiVG: centre lines, one even round-capped stroke, the same 2.5 px whatever the size, cropped to fill the box.

Proposal: cut each from Noto Serif JP itself, from a kanji that has it (the green strokes below), and fill it like text. Same weight (700), Mincho contrast, serifs, uroko and tapers; kept at its size in the host kanji and centred like the font's own parts. 㐄 is small in 降, so it's scaled 1.5× from a lighter weight (450) to keep the strokes as thick as the rest.
